FIFTEEN MEN WHO ONLY EVER COACHED

1. Charlie Brickley became Head Coach of the New York Brickley Giants in 1921. He died on December 28, 1949.

2. Bob Berryman spent one season as Head Coach of the BKN in 1926. In all he coached two seasons across two clubs, 1924 to 1926. He held the same job at the Frankford Yellow Jackets. He did not coach in 1925.

3. Jack Fish spent one season as Head Coach of the Newark Tornadoes in 1930. He died on June 29, 1971.

4. Ed Kubale spent one season as Head Coach of the Brooklyn Tigers in 1944. He died on February 4, 1971.

5. Andy Hewlett coached one season, 1947, with the Baltimore Colts, listed as Backfield.

6. Joe Sheeketski coached one season, 1951, with the New York Yanks, listed as Backfield.

7. Jack Hennemier spent two seasons as Head Coach of the Calgary Stampeders from 1955 to 1956.

8. jerry burns coached twenty-six seasons, 1966 to 1991, and was Head Coach of the Minnesota Vikings from 1986 to 1991. He coached for three clubs in all. He had been an assistant for twenty seasons before that.

9. Over twenty-seven seasons, 1969 to 1997, Marv Levy was Head Coach of the Buffalo Bills from 1986 to 1997. He coached for nine clubs in all. He was a head coach at four other clubs, among them the Montreal Alouettes, the Kansas City Chiefs and the Chicago Blitz. He was in and out of the game, missing 1983 and 1985. He had been an assistant for four seasons before that.

10. Kirk Mee coached from 1975 to 1977 with the Washington Redskins, most often listed as Special Assignments.

11. Roger Theder's coaching crossed leagues — the NFL and the USFL — most of it with the Baltimore Colts, later the Indianapolis Colts, listed as Running Backs. In all he coached five seasons across four clubs, 1982 to 1987. He did not coach in 1986.

12. Mike Borich coached from 1999 to 2000 with the Chicago Bears, listed as Wide Receivers.

13. Over eleven seasons, 2001 to 2026, Eric Price coached, most of it with the Jacksonville Jaguars, listed as Offense-Research. He coached for four clubs in all. He was in and out of the game, missing 2003–07, 2009–16 and 2021–22.

14. mike phair coached from 2018 to 2019 with the Indianapolis Colts.

15. Ramon Chinyoung coached from 2023 to 2024 with the Dallas Cowboys, listed as Assistant Offensive Line/Quality Control. In all he coached four seasons across three clubs, 2022 to 2025.

He played six seasons: for the Philadelphia Eagles from 1951 to 1952 and the Winnipeg Blue Bombers from 1953 to 1956. He was later Head Coach of the Minnesota Vikings from 1967 to 1983. He was a head coach at two other clubs too. He died on March 11, 2023.
