FIFTEEN MEN WHO ONLY EVER COACHED

1. Jack Keogh spent one season as Head Coach of the Hartford Blues in 1926. He died on February 13, 1955.

2. Alfred McGall spent one season as Head Coach of the Newark Tornadoes in 1930. He died on September 8, 1941.

3. Dudley DeGroot's coaching crossed leagues — the NFL and the AAFC — and its longest stretch was as Head Coach of the Los Angeles Dons from 1946 to 1947. In all he coached four seasons across two clubs, 1944 to 1947. He held the same job at the Washington Redskins. He died on May 5, 1970.

4. Jimmy Phelan coached in the AAFC and the NFL, most of it as Head Coach of the Los Angeles Dons from 1948 to 1949. In all he coached three seasons across two clubs, 1948 to 1952. He held the same job at the Dallas Texans. He was away from the game from 1950 to 1951. He died on November 14, 1974.

5. Lisle Blackbourn was Head Coach of the Green Bay Packers from 1954 to 1957. He died on June 14, 1983.

6. Over thirty-two seasons, 1963 to 1994, chuck knox was Head Coach of the Seattle Seahawks from 1983 to 1991. He coached for six clubs in all. He held the same job at the Los Angeles Rams, the Buffalo Bills and the Los Angeles Rams. He had been an assistant for ten seasons before that.

7. Over twenty-seven seasons, 1969 to 1997, Marv Levy was Head Coach of the Buffalo Bills from 1986 to 1997. He coached for seven clubs in all. He held the same job at the Montreal Alouettes, the Kansas City Chiefs and the Chicago Blitz. He was in and out of the game, missing 1983 and 1985. He had been an assistant for four seasons before that.

8. Chuck Studley coached twenty-three seasons, 1969 to 1991, and became Head Coach of the Houston Oilers in 1983. He coached for four clubs in all. He spent another twenty-two seasons as an assistant.

9. John McVay's coaching crossed leagues — the WFL and the NFL — and its longest stretch was as Head Coach of the New York Giants from 1976 to 1978. In all he coached five seasons across two clubs, 1974 to 1978. He held the same job at the Memphis Southmen.

10. Ron Erhardt spent four seasons as Head Coach of the New England Patriots from 1978 to 1981. He died on March 21, 2012.

11. John Mackovic was Head Coach of the Kansas City Chiefs from 1983 to 1986.

12. Richard Williamson was Head Coach of the Tampa Bay Buccaneers from 1990 to 1991. He died on September 21, 2015.

13. Kippy Brown became Head Coach of the Memphis Maniax in 2001.

14. John Fassel became Head Coach of the Los Angeles Rams in 2016.

15. Brian Daboll spent three seasons as Head Coach of the New York Giants from 2022 to 2024.
