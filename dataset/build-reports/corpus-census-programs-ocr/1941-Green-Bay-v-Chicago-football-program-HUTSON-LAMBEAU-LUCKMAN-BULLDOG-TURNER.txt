THE CHICAGO BEARS COACHING STAFF

HE coaching staff of the Chicago Bears are all former Bear players

—and good ones, too. George Halas, who organized the club in

1920 as the Staley's of Decatur, Ill, (the name was’ changed to

that of the Bears when the outfit moved to Wrigley Field in 1921), is

in his twenty-second year as president and his nineteenth as head coach.

He played end ten years and is a graduate of the University of Illinois.

Luke Johnsos retired in 1937 after seven years as an end with the

club and became assistant coach, tutoring the ends. Johnsos joined the

Bears after graduating from Northwestern University. His excellent play

won him all-league honors in 1933.

Hartley "Hunk" Anderson, is in his second year as line coach, He

was a guard on the 1922 team. Anderson's coaching career is long

and varied and he has coached at Notre Dame, St. Louis University,

North Carolina State College, Michigan, and the Detroit Lions. He is a

Notre Dame man.

John "Paddy" Driscoll was added to the staff this year when he

resigned as mentor at Marquette University. One of the all-time all-

star backs in the post-graduate circuit, he played six seasons with the

Cardinals and wasiwith the Bears from 1926 through 1929. He directs

the backfield. A Northwestern alumnus.
