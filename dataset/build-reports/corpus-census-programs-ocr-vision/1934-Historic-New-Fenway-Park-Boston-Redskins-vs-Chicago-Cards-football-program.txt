Foreword
Boston professional football_followers ean rightfully expect the gridiron
thriller of the season next Sunday afternoon when the ever-sensational Green Bay
Packers encounter the Redskins at Fenway Park. These celebrated Wisconsinites
feature a forward passing game that is perfection in itself, possessing the most
accurate of all passers in the colorful Arnold "Flash" Herber. On the receiving
end of these passes are such ends as Lavvie Dilweg, Al Rose and Milt Gantenbein.
The Packers have a wealth of star running backs including Bob Monnett, Clark
Hinkle, Hank Bruder, Joe Laws, Roger Grove and "Buckets" Goldenberg.
If you like that wide open game, and who doesn't, be on hand to see the
Green Bay Packers. And a final word of caution, buy your seats in advance. The
largest crowd of the season is sure to be in attendance.
