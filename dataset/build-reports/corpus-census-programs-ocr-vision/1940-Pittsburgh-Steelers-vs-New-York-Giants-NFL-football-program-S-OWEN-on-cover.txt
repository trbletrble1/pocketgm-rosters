Leemans Moves Up on Leaders
TUFFY LEEMANS, ace running back of the New York Giants, is now only
. a few yards behind Banks McFadden, Dodgers rookie ball carrier from
Clemson, in the race for ground gaining honors in the National Football League.
Leemans has gained 211 yards as against 239 yards for MeFadden although
Tuffy as you know only plays about half a game under the Giants two team
system.
Whizzer White is not far behind either. In fact, only seven yards behind
Leemans in third place but these three backs are the only ball carriers of the
circuit who have piled up over 200 yards. Two other Giants backs, Eddie
Miller and Walt Nielsen, are among the leaders however having gained over
100 yards apiece in four games.
Leemans in addition to his fine ground gaining record is setting a new
standard in passing for himself. Tuffy has completed 11 in 20 for a 55% mark
in efficiency, a mark surpassed only by Sammy Baugh who tops the league's
passers. The Redskins ace is well on his way to a new aerial mark having com-
pleted 40 in four games out of 57 attempted to gain 556 yards. Baugh and
Cecil Isbell of Green Bay have each completed five passes for touchdowns.
Ward Cuff is in the thick of the race for high scoring honors and is also
among the leaders in his specialty of field goal kicking. The Giants back, who
has led the league in place kicking the past two seasons, has kicked three field
goals. Armand Niccolai of the Pittsburgh Steelers and Clarke Hinkle, Green
Bay veteran, have each kicked four to top Cuff by one however. Niccolai's boot
from the 48 yard line against the Giants in Pittsburgh is the longest of the
season.
Cuff in addition to his field goal kicking has scored 20 points whieh gives
him fifth place in point getting behind Jimmy Johnston of the Redskins, Don
Looney of Philadelphia and Carl Mulleneaux, Green Bay end, all of them tied
at 24 points from four touchdowns.
