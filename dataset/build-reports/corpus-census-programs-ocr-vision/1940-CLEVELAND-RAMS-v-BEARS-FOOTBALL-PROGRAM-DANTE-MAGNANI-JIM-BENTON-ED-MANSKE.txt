Anderson, considered by most football experts as the great-
est strategist ever to teach this department of play. "Hunk,"
after an All-American career at Notre Dame, coached at
his alma mater, at North Carolina State, University of Michi-
gan, Cincinnati University, and "transferred" this present
season from the Detroit Lions. His working agreement with
Halas, years ago, was to play "pro" football on Sundays, and
to coach on weekdays.
Carl Brumbaugh, one of the lightest players to achieve
stardom in the big league, has retired from active competi-
tion—he played six years with the Bears—and will coach the
Halas quarterbacks this season.
