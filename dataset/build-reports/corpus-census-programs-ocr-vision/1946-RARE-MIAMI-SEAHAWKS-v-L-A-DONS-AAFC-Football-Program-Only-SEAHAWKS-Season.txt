Tonight's Game
They'll never decide the old controversy be-
tween the California orange and the Florida
orange, but tonight on the Coliseum turf the
supremacy of one brand of football over the
other should be well answered.
Both the Seahawks and the Dons are bring-
ing something brand new—major league sports
—to their respective states. And both clubs,
backed by prominent citizens, are determined
to give their states something besides climate,
bathing beauties, and orange juice to brag
about. The Seahawks have been treading a
rocky road and come into tonight's game as
underdogs, but no team with the constant
inspiration of Miss Muriel Smith, whose pic-
ture adorns this page, can be counted out.
Incidentally, Miss Smith, who will appear in
the between-half entertainment this evening,
is the former Miss Florida and was runner-up
to Miss America in 1943 and queen of the
Orange Bowl game in 1944. And just to prove
that the baton isn't merely a prop, Muriel won
the national drum majorette title in 1942.
The Dons hit the airways after tonight's
game and will be out of town for two weeks.
Next Sunday Coach Dud DeGroot's team
drops in at Buffalo to play the Bisons and then
flies back to Chicago for a game on October 4
against the Rockets. But we'll be home again
on October 12 to play San Francisco at Gilmore
Stadium.
MURIEL SMITH, Seahawk drum majorette.
