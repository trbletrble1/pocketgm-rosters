With today's game the Redskins begin their fifth season in Boston. The
season looms rosier than at any time in the team's history. Few replacements
have been made, but such new players as will be seen in the lineup are valuable
additions, the management feels. There is no elub in the National League
having so many brilliant stars in its roster as the Boston Redskins. Therefore
to seck for the eause of the rosiness of the outlook we modestly point to the
acquisition of a new head coach. In Ray Flaherty the management believes it
has aequired one of the most brilhant tacticians and sturdiest leaders in pro-
fessional football. A sterling veteran of the ranks and many times winner of
All-American honors in pro ranks, he comes rightfully to leadership. The
Redskins have been acclaimed as having at last manifested the power that was
theirs in the season's early games. The management asks its Boston publie to
forget past misdeeds and appraise the team for what it is and does this season.
And it bespeaks hearty encouragement for the team's efforts, feeling that due
pleasure will proceed from the team's playing this season.
