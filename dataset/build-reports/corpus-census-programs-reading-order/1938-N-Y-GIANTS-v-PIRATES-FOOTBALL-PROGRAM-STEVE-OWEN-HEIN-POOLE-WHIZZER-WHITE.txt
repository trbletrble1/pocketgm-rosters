PRO RULES
There are differences in rules for games
played in the National Football League and
under intercoliegiate rules. We would like to
call your attention to these major differences:
1. The goal posts are on the goal line (not
ten yards back).
2. Forward passes are permitted from any
point to the line of scrimmage (not thrown
from five yards behind line).
3. The penalty for a forward pass thrown
beyond the line of scrimmage is loss of down
and five yards from the spot such pass is made.
4. A forward pass on fourth down is not a
touchback if it goes over the goal line providing
the pass originated outside
In other words if the pass was thrown from
the 25-yard line on fourth down it reverts to
the defending team at that point.
5. On a kickoff the receiving team is not
required to have five players within five yards
of the restraining line.
to use tees in making kickoffs.
6. The penalty for clipping is 25 yards
(not 15).
7. The ball is not dead when the runner
touches the ground unless definitely in the
grasp of an opponent and all forward progress
stopped.
8. Substitutes may talk to teammates imme-
diately on entering game.
9. Any two players of either team may re-
enter the game once in the fourth quarter.
10. When a penalty is inflicted inside the
10-yard line it shall be only half the distance
to the goal line.
