THE NATIONAL FOOTBALL LEAGUE
Following are the major differences in rules be-
tween professional and college football games:
The goal posts are placed on the goal line,
The ball must be put in play at least 15 yards in
from the sideline, being moved 15 yards in after
any play that ends closer than 15 yards to the
nearest sideline.
The penalty for clipping from behind is 25 yards.
The ball is NOT dead when a player slips or is
thrown to the ground, the ball being in play until
the whistle is blown.
Defensive linemen are allowed to use their hands
on the head, face or bodies of offensive players.
Fumbles, except on punts or lateral passes, may
be advanced. Punts and lateral pass fumbles may
be advanced only after "possession and advance"
has been established.
Substitutes may talk with teammates immediate-
ly upon entering the game.
A forward pass may be thrown from any spot
behind the line of scrimmage.
On a forward pass followed by an illegal lateral
pass (the second pass being forward instead of
lateral or backward) the ball is put in play at a
point 5 yards behind where the illegal pass was
thrown.
On the kickoff the receiving team does NOT have
to have five men within its 45 yard line.
The flying block and flying tackle are permitted.
Penalties within the ten-yard line are only half
the distance to the goal line.

Green Bay Packers on the Way
The high spot of the New York Giants home
stand is on tap next Sunday when the Green
Bay Packers invade the Polo Grounds for
their annual game with the Owen outfit. The
Packers come East this season with an even
greater record than a year ago when they went
on to win the National League championship.
Minus the services of Arnold (Flash) Herber
and Bob Monnett, their two forward passing
experts, the Packers dropped their first two
games this fall. Since that time, however,
Herber and Monnett have been back in action
much to the dismay of all opposition.
The Packers now boast a string of six con-
secutive wins, the longest streak of the season,
in fact the longest of several seasons in the
pro grid circuit. In rolling up that string of
victories the Green Bay outfit has rolled up
the astounding total of 168 points, and that
performance was done against such opposition
as the Chicago Cardinals, Detroit Lions,
Chicago Bears and the strongest clubs of the
circuit.

last year set a new record for the league. The
