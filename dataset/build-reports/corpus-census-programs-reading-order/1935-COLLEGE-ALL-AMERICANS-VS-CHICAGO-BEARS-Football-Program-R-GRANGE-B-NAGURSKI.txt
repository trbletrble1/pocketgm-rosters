atmosphere. The patrons of tonight's game are cordially invited
to visit the wierd catacombs in the bowels of the earth, where

Cellarage is stocked with rare wines and Allan-a-Dale's Min-
strels play tantalizing tunes for those wishing to dance in Sher-
wood Forest. Then, for those who wish to sit by a waterfall
under starry skies and quaff a delicious mint julep, may find
this romantic atmosphere in our beautiful outdoor garden. The
Ivanhoe extends its best wishes to both college and professional
football and may the better team win.
