1943 Season
Today's contest between the Chicago Cardinals and
Green Bay Packers introduces Philip Handler as the new
Cardinals' master-mind. "Motz," as he is known to his
legion of friends, presents a squad which has seventeen
Veteran backs returning, those whose names are best
recognized by South Side fans, are Marshall Goldberg,
Johnny Hall, Buddy Parker, Johnny Martin and Bob Mor-
row. The latter has been converted from a fullback into
a blocking back and Martin has been moved to left end.
Veteran forwards are Guards Conway Baker, starting
his eighth season, and Gordon Wilson, now in his second
year, and Tackles Chester Bulger and Gilford Duggan,
Outstanding among the new men are Backs Joe Bukant,
Johnny Grigas and Ronnie Cahill, and Linemen Don Cur-
rivan and Ed Rucinski at ends; Art Albrecht at tackle,
and Floyd Rhea and Marshall Robnett at the guards.
Bukant saw little action with the club last season but
this year figures to be in most of the games most of the
Each year the Cardinals seem to come up with an out-
standing rookie and this year appears to be no exception
due to the presence of End Don Currivan. Currivan, All-
American performer while at Boston College last year,
looms as a candidate for the "Rookie of the Year" honors.
Predictions—what will happen as the season
pro-
gresses are out of order this year. The Cards to date
have scored 38 points, an average of 19 points per game.
Unfortunately the defense hasn't been on a par with the
offense and, let it be known here and now, that situation
is no fault of Coach Handler's. The latter has always
been a great defensive coach. Some of the Cardinal lines
during the past five or six years have been "honeys" and
with a little strength at left end and center, it is safe to
say that the 1943 line would give a good account of itself.
One thing is certain, the current edition of Cardinals
will give everybody the best they've got—they are that
