Following are the major differences in rules be-

The penalty for clipping from behind is 25 yards.
The ball is NOT dead when a player slips or is
thrown to the ground, the ball being in play until
Defensive linemen are allowed to use their hands
on the head, face or bodies of offensive players.
Fumbles, except on punts or lateral passes, may
be advanced. Punts and lateral pass fumbles may
be advanced only after "possession and advance"
Substitutes may talk with teammates immediate-
A forward pass may be thrown from any spot
On a forward pass followed by an illegal lateral
pass (the second pass being forward instead of
lateral or backward) the ball is put in play at a
point 5 yards behind where the illegal pass was
On the kickoff the receiving team does NOT have
The flying block and flying tackle are permitted.
