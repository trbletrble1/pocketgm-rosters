James Spence Authentication, LLC (JSA) offers several different forms of authenticity which includes a service specifically designed for approved auction houses.
These Auction House Letters of Authenticity (AHLOA) are exclusively created to accommodate national auction houses keeping in mind the large volume of signature
material presented in a specific catalogue. The winning bidders receive our AHLOA on our proprietary watermarked paper containing the lot number, item(s)
description (per the auction house) and date of the offering. These AHLOA differ from our regular LOA's in as much as they are preliminary reviews of the items and
do not go through the rigors of our full examination process. As our examination of auction lots is cursory and limited, JSA is not responsible if any lot or items
in a lot are subsequently determined to be not authentie or are inconelusive. In addition, JSA does not grade items and specifically disclaims any
responsibility therefore. Unlike the regular JSA LOA, the AHLOA's do not contain the live notarized signature of James Spence, a high resolution digital sean of the
signature(s), certification number and sequential sticker, nor our own specific description of the offered lot. The text provided by the auction house is solely their
verbiage. If the winning auction bidder elects to have the AHLOA upgraded to our regular service, JSA charges a SS0 upgrade fee per individual item. This service is
recommended by JSA and will ensure the highest level of our opined expertise. In the rare event that our opinion differs from our preliminary conclusion with the
auction house, no upgrade fee will be charged. Only original AHLOAs are accepted for our upgraded service. AHLOA's are non-transferable and will only be honored
for upgraded service to the original lot winner. Phofocopies are not accepted. It is important for all of our customers to fully understand the differences in our services
