I hopeful of continuing their new role of
conquering heroes. With five straight vic-
tories the flashy Maramen are comfortably
ensconced on top in the National League's
Eastern Division and will battle to the last
inch to retain their lofty position.
In the Giants' scheme of operations today
also enters the matter of revenge. Coach
Steve Owen and his veterans, and even the
large crop of brilliant rookies, all have on
their minds how the Dodgers defeated their
Inter-Borough foemen 14-6 before over 50,000
wild-eyed fans in the 1940 finale in the Polo
Grounds.
Coaches Steve and Bill Owen and Mike Palm
have worked long and diligently for this occa-
sion. They were actuated by the same mo-
tive to square accounts with Coach Jock
Sutherland's pupils. But at no time did to-
day's invaders fall into the usually fatal mis-
take of becoming over-confident.
