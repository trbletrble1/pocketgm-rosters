## Per club-season, and 26 codes prove why

Every StatsCrew roster page title carries the club's name **for that season**:
`1924 Minneapolis Marines football Roster`. So the whole corpus is covered, not
just the 418 PFA pages I had proposed.

**Stored as `("club_season", league, year, team)` → `club_name`** — a fact about
the season, not about a franchise. Whether two club-seasons are the same
franchise is the question §5.2 deliberately keeps separate, and this does not
answer it.

That is not a technicality. **26 team codes carry more than one name:**

| code | names |
|---|---|
| `BU1` | Buffalo All-Americans → Buffalo Bisons → **Buffalo Rangers** |
| `AKR` | Akron Pros → Akron Indians |
| `BO3` | Boston Braves → Boston Redskins |
| `BRO` | Brooklyn Dodgers → Brooklyn Tigers |
| `BO4` | Boston Yanks → **Boston Yanks/Brooklyn Tigers** |

One name per code would have been wrong for part of 26 clubs' histories. And the
merged wartime names survive intact — the bios now render *"the Philadelphia
Eagles/Pittsburgh Steelers"*, which is the 1943 Steagles, correctly, because it
is what that season's page says.

## And a factual error fixed, not a cosmetic one

> **"Mike McCarthy … played 18 seasons from 2006 to 2024."**

He never played. A coaching stint and a playing stint are the same `stint`
subject, told apart only by a `role_title` claim the sentence-builder consulted
too late. Now:

> **Mike McCarthy**, born November 10, 1963, **appears in the record as a coach,
> not a player.** He coached 18 seasons between 2006 and 2024, for the Green Bay
> Packers, the Dallas Cowboys. He came from Baker. **He appears on a staff in
> 2018, then not again until 2020.**

The phrasing is deliberate — *appears in the record as* rather than *was*. Greasy
Neale gets the same sentence, and he did play, in the 1910s, for a league this
archive does not hold. **The claim is about the record, not about the man**, which
is the only thing the archive can honestly assert.
