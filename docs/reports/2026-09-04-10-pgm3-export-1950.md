# Exporting 1950 to PocketGM 3 — what the consumer's gates actually measure

**2026-09-04. Report 10.** §10 step 7. Follows report 09.
Manifest at `dataset/build-reports/manifest-1950.json`.

---

## The headline number

**The consumer needs 52 keys. The dataset supplies six.**

    from the dataset   forename, surname, position, teamID, teamNum, age
    invented here      12,910 values across 13 fields

That ratio is the design working, not failing. The dataset holds what is known
about 1950 — who played, where, at what position, born when — and **every rating,
attribute, salary, contract, appearance and growth curve in the exported file was
invented by the export and declared in its manifest.** None of it was written back.

446 records, 13 clubs, zero refusals.

## What the export chose, because the dataset abstained

**26 choices recorded.** Three kinds:

**1. A contested franchise, and the §9.5 gate fired first.** The export refuses to
run if a club has no `lineage_choices` entry — no fallback, no first-match, no
null. **NYY, the 1950 New York Yanks, is the contested one:** the lineage usually
runs Yanks → Dallas Texans → Baltimore Colts → Indianapolis, but whether the 1953
Colts continued that franchise or were an expansion club is not settled in any
source held here. New York's slots are taken by NYG and NYJ, so city is
unavailable and lineage must be used — which is exactly where the contest lives.

Exported to `IND` **under protest**, with the dissent recorded: if the 1953 Colts
were new, the Yanks lineage terminates in 1952 and this club has no modern slot at
all. **The dataset still holds it unresolved.** This one wants a ruling.

**2. Thirteen mid-season movers.** The dataset holds both stints; PGM3 has one
team field. The export picks the club with more games played and records the
margin. No coin flip in the dataset — only in the build, where it belongs.

**3. Eleven contested positions**, from report 09 — the movers whose two clubs
list them differently in the era-native vocabulary. The export emits the first
candidate and records what it discarded. `p_000010` went to `QB` over `QB-K`.

## What was lost in translation, counted

The position map turns 103 era-native codes into PGM3's 15. Every composite code
keeps its first token and **the manifest counts what was dropped** — 27 distinct
losses, e.g. `secondary position tokens (LOG kept): 6`. Two-way players lose a
side. A middle guard becomes a `DT` and loses the linebacker half. **Zero
refusals**, meaning the map covered every code it met; a token with no entry
raises rather than falling through.

Six jersey numbers collided within a club after movers were assigned and were
reassigned, with the originals recorded as lost.

## The validator result, and the split that matters

First run: **14 check groups failed.** After fixing the export's own defects:
**four.** The split is the useful output.

### Fixed — my invention was wrong

| failure | cause | fix |
|---|---|---|
| `appearance token not in vocab` [446] | I invented a `Skin1a` token that does not exist | slot vocabularies derived from the reference |
| `duplicate jersey within team` [6] | movers brought their number | de-duplicate within club, losses recorded |
| `median team payroll $73.8M vs $196.4–198.4M` | era-accurate dollars leave the financial layer inert | one uniform ×2.2930 — now **$197.4M**, the published constant to the dollar |
| `guarantee/salary should rise with length` | drawn at random | guarantee tracks **remaining** length |
| `rookie contract ladder not descending` | length unrelated to draft year | 4/3/2/1 by years pro |
| `cross-year medians` (14 attributes) | attributes drawn at rating ±14, median 65 vs reference 83 | **level from the reference, order from the rating** — the roster project's own rule |
| `zero-pattern` | I filled every attribute for every position | **position-gated** — only the attributes a position actually uses |

### Remaining — and none is a defect in the file

| gate | reads | the 1950 fact |
|---|---|---|
| `team count != 32` | 19 slots empty | the NFL had **13 clubs** |
| `roster under 45` | 13 clubs short | the 1950 limit was **32**; median exported roster is **33** |
| `CB/S ratio 2.92 vs 1.11–1.13` | too many corners | the 1950 secondary is two **defensive halfbacks** and one safety |
| `team empty at a position` | no K, P, MLB on some clubs | only **9 of 13** clubs listed a punter; the 5-2 front used a **middle guard**, not a middle linebacker |

**Every one of those gates was fitted on files from 1986 to 2026.** They encode
modern football's positional structure and roster size, and they are right for the
population they were built on. Pointed at 1950 they measure the era and report it
as failure.

**This is report 06's per-league finding on a second axis.** There, a completeness
gate scoped per era read the WFL as broken because games-played fill is a property
of the *league*. Here, positional gates scoped across all files read 1950 as broken
because positional structure is a property of the *era*. Written up as a precedent:
**a gate carries the population it was fitted on, and refuses or widens outside
it.**

**And the four failures are a finding, not a nuisance** — they are a list of the
ways 1950 football differs from modern football, produced automatically by pointing
a modern instrument at it.

## Does the design have a hole?

**Not one that this found.** The three things the master session asked to see:

- **Four §9.6 bases distinct in real data** — report 09, proven across NFL 1950
  and WFL 1974, with state 1 and state 2 co-occurring in `games_started`.
- **Identity through slugs, cross-reference followed** — 32 men with one person id
  and two careers; the matching slug body was never treated as evidence.
- **A contested value surviving the round trip** — 11 contested positions went in
  contested, stayed contested in the store, and were decided **by the export** with
  the discarded candidate recorded. The store still says `contested`.

The export is demanding exactly as promised, and what it demanded was **invention**,
not information the dataset was withholding. That is the layering working: the
dataset said "I do not know Sisto Averno's speed", the export made a number up, and
the manifest says so.
